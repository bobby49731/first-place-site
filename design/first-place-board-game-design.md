# First Place — Board Game Design

**Format:** 44-square perimeter board (4 corners + 10 squares per side), single pass — no repeated laps. One journey from Move-In Day to The Dream, Realized.

**Characters:** Fran, Jeremy, Will, Martha — same board layout for everyone; only the "Story Beat" and "Roomie" squares swap in character-specific flavor.

**Core mechanics locked in:**
- Verification: honor-system for private actions (e.g. "email 3 friends" — simple confirm button), trackable share links for public actions (social posts)
- Single-player vs. the board (not racing other real players)
- Pacing: flexible — a few moves per visit, fine if someone finishes in one sitting
- Roomie collectibles: 8 total squares (2 chances per character), found by landing on "Find a Roomie" squares
- Roomie Gate (Square 23): need at least 2 Roomies collected to pass — if you don't have them, you're sent back to find more
- Finish reward: reaching Square 44 reveals that character's "dream, realized" moment + a direct pledge link into their product card. Collecting all 4 Roomies unlocks a bonus/"perfect completion" moment.

**Movement rule (prevents infinite/confusing chains):**
Only the square you land on via your move/spin triggers its effect. If that effect moves your token again (advance/move back), the destination square's own effect does NOT re-trigger — you simply land there and your turn ends. This is the same rule Chutes and Ladders uses, and it means the board doesn't need to be hand-tuned to avoid every possible collision between advance/move-back squares.

**Bug fix from initial draft:** Square 43 ("Advance 3") would overshoot past the Finish at Square 44. Movement should always cap at Square 44 — if a move would go past it, the token simply lands on 44 (Finish) instead of overshooting.

**Reset rule:** When caught by The Doubt (see below), the player's board position resets to Square 1 — but any Roomies already collected are KEPT, not lost. This keeps real stakes without punishing someone out of the game entirely.

## Roomie Collectible Squares (per-character, 8 chances = 2 per Roomie)

**Important correction from initial draft:** Since the player picks ONE character to play as, Roomie squares award items from THAT character's own set of 4 Roomies — not one borrowed from each of the other three characters. The board layout (which squares are Roomie squares) is identical for everyone; only which item gets awarded differs based on who you're playing as.

Squares 5, 15, 26, and 37 are Roomie squares. Since there are 4 Roomies per character and 4 Roomie squares on the board, each landing could award a random not-yet-collected Roomie from your character's set, with a second full pass giving repeat chances if the board is played more than once (or simply: first 4 landings work through the set in order, any landings after that once complete could re-award duplicates or convert to a small bonus).

## The Villain: "The Doubt"

The personification of low expectations and self-doubt — the thing the show is actually about pushing back against. Not a scoring gimmick; its movement is directly tied to the player's real actions, reinforcing the show's message mechanically, not just narratively.

**Visual identity:** A shadowy, gray, deflating figure — visually drained of color against the site's warm, hand-painted illustration style, so it reads as "wrong" the moment it appears on the board. Physically: tall and lanky, strong build, a conventionally good-looking face undercut by the strongest, squarest jaw imaginable — striking to the point of being unsettling. The confidence of that jawline is the point: doubt doesn't look uncertain, it looks like it's never questioned itself for a second, in direct contrast to the player's own real uncertainty.

**Voice lines (shown when it's near/gaining ground):**
- "You'll never finish this."
- "Nobody's really watching."
- "You can't do this alone."

**Movement:**
- Starts a few squares behind the player at game start (suggested: 5 squares back)
- Every player turn, The Doubt creeps forward a small fixed amount automatically (suggested: 1 square) — constant, quiet pressure
- Land on a SETBACK square → The Doubt gets an extra move forward (setbacks feed doubt)
- Land on a CONNECTION square → The Doubt gets pushed BACK (suggested: 2 squares) — real connection with others weakens it
- Milestone and Story Beat squares: no effect on The Doubt's position (neutral)

**Catch condition:** If The Doubt's position ever equals or passes the player's position, it catches them.

**On catch:** Player's board position resets to Square 1. Roomies already collected are kept. The Doubt also resets to its starting distance behind the player.

## Finish Reward (Square 44)

- Reveals that character's real dream photo + a short "the dream, realized" story beat
- Direct "Pledge now to help make this real" link straight into that character's product card
- If all 4 Roomies were collected along the way: unlock a bonus "perfect completion" moment (extra content, or a special badge/acknowledgment)
